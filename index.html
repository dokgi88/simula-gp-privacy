Simula GP — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Simula GP ("the app") is a game that runs entirely on your device. The optional Wi-Fi Race mode connects only to other devices on the same local network.

  • No personal data is collected. The app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.
  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and does not use advertising identifiers.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases. The app shows no advertising and contains no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No internet connection is required or used. The app sends nothing to any server. It asks for local-network access (iOS "Local Network", Android network permissions) only so Wi-Fi Race can find and join rooms.
  • Wi-Fi Race (optional): when you create or join a room, the app talks only to other devices on the same local Wi-Fi network. It shares your team's name, colours, car design, car performance and driver names and ratings with the players in that room, for that race only. Nothing is stored on their device after the race and nothing goes over the internet.
  • Saves stay on your device. Your career and settings are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. Device backups (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include it, under the terms of your device provider.
  • Steam. If you play the Steam version, your purchase, your Steam account and the optional Steam Cloud save sync are handled by Valve under Steam's own privacy policy; the game itself still collects nothing.
  • Children. Because no data is collected, the app collects no data from children either.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
